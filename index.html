TEAM VEGHA / TKMCE
ELECTRIC SCOOTER DESIGN TEAM

Team VEGHA, the electric scooter design team of TKM College of Engineering, driven by innovation, technical excellence, and a passion for sustainable mobility.

Competing in prestigious national events conducted by SAE India, the team brings together talented engineering students who collaborate to design, develop, and optimise electric two-wheelers that represent the future of mobility.

SAE India provides a platform for students to showcase their capabilities in design, manufacturing, and real-world engineering problem-solving. For Team VEGHA, this stage is more than a competition — it is an opportunity to transform theoretical knowledge into practical solutions, demonstrate innovative thinking, and build industry-ready engineering skills.

01

Our mission is to shape the future of urban mobility by creating electric scooters that are efficient, affordable, and environmentally responsible. We aim to bridge the gap between technological innovation and sustainability at a time when the world is urgently seeking clean transportation solutions.

02

ENGINEER A
HEALTHIER PLANET.

Team Vegha is committed to developing green mobility technologies that minimise carbon emissions, reduce dependence on fossil fuels, and contribute to a healthier planet. Through continuous learning, engineering excellence, and responsible innovation, we strive to design vehicles that set new standards in performance, reliability, and ecofriendly transportation.
